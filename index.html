Understanding Facial Features

A simple guide to facial appearance and individual features.

Facial Symmetry

Facial symmetry describes how similar the two sides of a face appear. Small differences between the two sides are completely natural and are part of what makes every face unique.

Facial Proportions

Facial proportions describe the relationship between different parts of the face. The position and size of features such as the eyes, nose, lips, and chin can influence the overall appearance.

Jawline

The jawline gives structure to the lower part of the face. Its shape naturally varies between people and can contribute to the overall character of a face.

Smile

A smile can change the way the face appears. The lips, teeth, cheeks, and the area around the eyes all work together to create a facial expression.

Skin Quality

Skin is another visible part of facial appearance. Texture, tone, and overall skin condition can affect how facial features appear under different lighting conditions.

Every Face Is Different

There is no single feature that defines someone's appearance. Facial features work together in different ways, creating a unique combination for every person.
